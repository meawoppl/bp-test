C333
C333 is a 22 uF, 50 V rail bypass connected across VBAT_24V and GND.
Its 1812 footprint is smaller than the 2220 parts in C335-C342, the eight-part
22 uF reservoir bank. That bank totals 176 uF nominal. C333 adds another
22 uF nominal in parallel; it does not perform voltage conversion or series
battery combining. C333 and the bank are unchanged. Exact capacitor part
selection and effective capacitance under DC bias remain unqualified.

CONNECTIONS
Both connectors now appear together in BATTERY INPUTS / SERIES LINK +
RESERVOIR. They were already adjacent on the PCB and remain in place.

Two separate 12 V batteries:
  J4.1  = upper battery positive = VBAT_24V
  J4.2  = upper battery negative = BAT_MID_12V
  J30.1 = lower battery positive = BAT_MID_12V
  J30.2 = lower battery negative = board GND

The PCB makes the J4.2-J30.1 series link. The upper battery negative must
remain separate from board ground; tying both battery negatives together
would short the lower battery through the series link.

One 24 V PSU, instead of the batteries:
  PSU positive to J4.1; PSU negative to J30.2.
  Leave J4.2 and J30.1 unused. No jumper is needed.
