# Component 3D models

All **286 fitted components** have existing, project-local STEP references. The other thirteen footprints are five mounting holes and eight bare test pads; their geometry comes from the board itself. Full inventory: [3d-model-audit.json](3d-model-audit.json).

Standard ICs, passives, headers and terminal blocks use KiCad package models, copied into `../3dmodels/`. J1/J2 retain the existing Hirose DF40 mating-connector STEP models. These generic package models are suitable for visualization, not an exact mechanical certification for every unresolved BOM item.

U30/U31 and L40/L60 now use **drawing-derived STEP envelopes**, replacing VRML-only stand-ins:

- `LMZ14202H_envelope.step`: TI NDW0007A drawing in [LMZ14202H.pdf](datasheets/LMZ14202H.pdf), page 31. Nominal body 9.85 × 10.16mm, overall lead-to-body extent 13.77mm, body height 4.57mm. Seven simplified leads, exposed underside ground and visible pin-1 mark. Mold draft and lead bends are simplified.
- `WCM4532F2SF_envelope.step`: TAI-TECH drawing in [WCM4532F2SF-900T20.pdf](datasheets/WCM4532F2SF-900T20.pdf), page 2. Nominal 4.5 × 3.2 × 2.8mm package and four terminals. Internal winding/core detail is omitted.

These two envelopes are generated by `tools/hardware/tesla_model_envelopes.py` using CadQuery; they are not manufacturer-supplied CAD. Run with `tmp/models/venv/bin/python`. Standard KiCad models retain their upstream provenance/license; custom envelopes are project-generated.

`tools/hardware/tesla_models.py` attaches the STEP files to both the active board and its local footprint library. It verifies paths, counts coverage and asserts that pad positions, net assignments and footprint placement remain unchanged. The main board constructor now reattaches edited KiCad model objects explicitly, avoiding the SWIG-copy behavior that previously left system-library paths in place. Floorplan and choke generators retain STEP references on regeneration.

Visual review: [oblique render](models-oblique.png). Checked module lead orientation/pin 1, choke terminal alignment, bank spacing and connector orientation. No obvious body interference in the current floorplan. This is component-model coverage, not a full enclosure, mating-cable or mounted-daughterboard assembly clearance approval.

Native ERC and physical DRC/parity remain zero. The board still has at least 499 reported unrouted items; no fabrication outputs are published for the development layout.
