# Support placement revision

The right edge keeps USB, trigger input, trigger output and GNSS SMA centers at y=30/50/70/90 mm. Support circuitry is arranged inward from each connector:

- USB ESD, attach/current detection, CC supply and input power switching occupy the upper right. The logic regulator and its input/output capacitors sit below the power section.
- Trigger comparator, 1 nF input filter, threshold divider and translator sit immediately inward from the input SMA. The output buffer and series resistor align with the output SMA.
- The OCXO buck and its inductor/output caps are grouped beside the oscillator, separated from the trigger comparator.
- GNSS supply bypassing and antenna bias support remain near the receiver; the 9.21 mm straight RF feed is unchanged.

The canonical routed PCB is authoritative for copper; docs/circuit.json and
final-placements.json record the current component placements. The 2026-09-27
cleanup aligns regulator input caps to supply-pin rows, forms a compact trigger
threshold column, aligns the output series resistor with its SMA, and tightens the
OCXO feedback divider. C70/C71/C72 have short explicit ground-return connections
and nearby vias outside their pads and package bodies. The buck power-switching
node remains short and the feedback routing stays below it.

Native refilled DRC now reports zero violations, zero unconnected items and zero
schematic parity issues. ERC is unchanged/clean. Native rotated-pad checks find no
via-drill overlaps; connector locations, USB paths, GNSS RF feed, OCXO G6 input,
and the newly ordered display bus are preserved. Review details and remaining
board-wide heuristic findings are in passive-tidy-review.md.

The module body outline is grouped with J1/J2/H1 and regenerated by tools/hardware/calibrator_module_boundary.py. Its exact F.Fab envelope is x145..165, y10..55 mm. A three-sided silkscreen outline identifies the body on the carrier; the top coincides with the carrier edge.

The OLED uses a 3 mm mounting gap; see display-mounting.md. Four 4 mm-diameter spacer envelopes were checked against neighboring footprint bodies. The closest unrelated footprint bounding box is over 3.3 mm from a mounting-hole center (spacer radius 2 mm). Actual OLED underside parts and header engagement require first-article dry fit.
