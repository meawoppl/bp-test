U1 is AD7606C-16BSTZ. Its rotation places pins 1–16 along the bottom.
The six bottom pins on +3V3 are configuration inputs:
  3/4/5 OS0/OS1/OS2: 111 selects software mode.
  6 PAR/SER SEL: high selects serial operation.
  7 STBY: held high; ignored in software mode.
  10 WR: unused parallel-interface control, held inactive high.
RANGE pin 8 is grounded (ignored in software mode); unused DB0 pin 16
is grounded. These are fixed digital levels, not additional supplies.

On this board, pin 1 is the bottom edge's +5V_ADC supply input, with
C1 100nF. Pins 37/38/48 share +5V_ADC with C2/C3/C4 100nF and C10 10uF.
VDRIVE pin 23 uses +3V3 and C5 100nF. The shared +3V3 routing joins the
configuration straps, VDRIVE, and REF SELECT pin 34. Its winding shape
is a routing choice, not a requirement of the selected operating mode.

Special-node checks on the saved board:
  REGCAP pins 36 and 39 each connect only to their own 1uF capacitor
  (C6 and C7 respectively), separate from each other and supply rails.
  REFIN/OUT pin 42 connects to C8 100nF.
  REFCAPA/B pins 44/45 share the C9 10uF node, separate from supplies.
